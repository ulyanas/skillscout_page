Do I need it

Skillscout is designed for AI builders who want to find the right skills quickly – without context-switching into another tab, another search, or another tool.

Reads page context

Reads the active site and derives a useful keyword from the hostname and page context.

Finds best skills

Searches supported catalogs and ranks results across relevance, popularity, and trust signals.

Keeps you moving

Shows the top results right in the popup, with a direct path to the original skill page and more results when you need them.
